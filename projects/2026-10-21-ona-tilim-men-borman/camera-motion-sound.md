## Motion design
- 3D roots/tree: gold filaments → roots → tree.
- 3D text: build from real typography, extrude/bevel, restrained metallic reflections.
- Typography: KIMLIGIMIZ / TARIXIMIZ / ERTANGI KUNIMIZ.
- Final card: 21-OKTABR / ONA TILIM — MILLATIMNING RUHI.
- Keep captions within platform-safe areas.
- Avoid illegible AI-generated Uzbek text.

## Sound design
- 00–10: low ambient, heartbeat, breath, reverse swell, one short impact.
- 10–20: page turn, pen, sparse piano, gentle lullaby texture.
- 20–30: subtle particles, organic rumble, soft crystalline tone.
- 30–42: restrained strings, controlled whoosh, metallic hit, gradual crescendo.
- 42–54: voice forward, music ducking, minimal low impacts.
- 54–60: music drops under final line, natural voice tail, soft resolve.

## Original music generation prompt
Compose an original 60-second cinematic score for a short film celebrating the Uzbek language. Begin with intimate low ambient tones and sparse piano, introduce subtle warm string textures during heritage scenes, build into a restrained orchestral crescendo during the direct-to-camera speech, and end with a powerful but understated resolve. Use delicate plucked-string textures inspired by Central Asian timbres without cliché. Leave clear space for spoken Uzbek narration. No vocals, no lyrics, no overpowering percussion, no excessive trailer booms. Dignified, emotional, culturally grounded, memorable.
