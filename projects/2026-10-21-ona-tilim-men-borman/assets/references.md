- Qahramonlar uchun bu rasmlardagi odamlarning yuzini nusxalamang. Uchta qahramon uchun alohida original character reference yarating.

- Har bir yangi mavzu uchun o‘sha mavzuga tegishli alohida `references.md` yarating.
